Theoretical Foundations, Graph Theory & Math

Built on seminal research in distributed systems, transactional consistency, probabilistic scheduling, and delta-debugging.

Adya (1999) & Berenson (1995) CSR Theorem

Direct Serialization Graph DSG(S)

A concurrent schedule \(S\) over transactions \(\mathcal{T} = \{T_1, \dots, T_n\}\) is Conflict Serializable (CSR) if and only if its directed conflict graph \(DSG(S) = (V, E)\) is a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG):

S ∈ CSR ⇔ DSG(S) has no directed cycles
  • P4 (Lost Update): \(T_1 \xrightarrow{rw} T_2 \xrightarrow{ww} T_1\) (\(G\text{-single}\))
  • A5B (Write Skew): \(T_1 \xrightarrow{rw} T_2 \xrightarrow{rw} T_1\) (Fekete Dangerous Structure)
  • A5A (Read Skew): \(T_1 \xrightarrow{rw} T_2 \xrightarrow{wr} T_1\) (\(G\text{-skew}\))
  • G0 (Dirty Write): \(T_1 \xrightarrow{ww} T_2 \xrightarrow{ww} T_1\) (\(G\text{-write}\))
  • G1c (Circular Info): \(T_1 \xrightarrow{wr} T_2 \xrightarrow{wr} T_1\)
  • G2 (Anti-Dependency): \(T_1 \xrightarrow{rw} T_2 \xrightarrow{rw} T_3 \xrightarrow{rw} T_1\)
Burckhardt et al. (ASPLOS 2010) PCT-SQL

Probabilistic Concurrency Testing

For \(n\) concurrent worker threads executing \(k\) operations with a bug of synchronization depth \(d\), the PCT scheduler guarantees a rigorous lower bound on bug detection probability:

&Pop;[Detection] ≥ 1 / (n · kd-1)

With combinatorial interleaving space \(|\Omega| = \frac{(K \cdot M)!}{(M!)^K} \approx 168\text{M}\) permutations, ChaosSQL's stochastic micro-jitter \(d_i \sim \mathcal{U}(\delta_{\min}, \delta_{\max})\) forces thread preemption at exact critical points without exhaustive brute force.

Andreas Zeller (IEEE TSE 2002) < 200ms Active Shrink

Causal Delta-Debugging (ddmin)

Given a failing trace sequence \(C = \langle op_1, \dots, op_N \rangle\) where \(\text{test}(C) = \text{FAIL}\), ChaosSQL synthesizes an isolated 1-minimal reproduction \(C^* \subseteq C\):

test(C*) = FAIL ∧ ∀ op ∈ C*: test(C* \ {op}) = PASS

Binary recursive bisection collapses 20-100 noisy operations down to the exact 2 operations in \(O(n \log n)\) average time, generating self-contained, zero-dependency repro_test.go files for instant regression testing.
